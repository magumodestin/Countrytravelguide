Australia

Canberra is the capital city of Australia, located in the Australian Capital Territory (ACT). It's a planned city, designed by American architect Walter Burley Griffin in 1912.

Must-see tourist attractions:

  1. Parliament House: The seat of Australia's federal government, offering guided tours.
  2. Australian War Memorial: A museum showcasing Australia's military history.
  3. National Museum of Australia: A museum exploring Australia's history, culture & society.
  4. Lake Burley Griffin: A picturesque lake in the heart of the city.
  5. Black Mountain Tower: Panoramic views of the city.

Melbourne & Sydney are iconic with Federation Square, Opera House, and stunning beaches.

Canada

Canada is known for vast landscapes, diverse culture, and friendly people.

  1. Niagara Falls: One of the most famous waterfalls in the world.
  2. Banff National Park: Beautiful park in the Canadian Rockies.
  3. CN Tower: Iconic tower in Toronto with amazing views.

Brazil

Largest country in South America, vibrant culture and Amazon rainforest.

  1. Christ the Redeemer: Iconic statue in Rio de Janeiro.
  2. Iguazu Falls: One of the largest waterfall systems.
  3. Amazon Rainforest: World's largest tropical rainforest.

Germany

Berlin is Germany's capital, full of history and modern energy.

  • Berlin Wall Memorial
  • Brandenburg Gate
  • Reichstag Building
  • Vibrant nightlife

United States Of America

New York City: Statue of Liberty, Central Park, Metropolitan Museum.

Los Angeles: Hollywood, Universal Studios, Santa Monica Pier.

Las Vegas: The Strip, Bellagio Fountains, Red Rock Canyon.

Argentina

Buenos Aires – tango, European architecture, La Boca, Recoleta Cemetery.

Patagonia: Perito Moreno Glacier, Fitz Roy. Iguazú Falls, Mendoza wine region.

France

Paris – Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame, Champs-Élysées, Montmartre. Also visit Versailles, French Riviera.

Japan

Tokyo – Skytree, Senso-ji, Shibuya Crossing, Meiji Shrine, Tsukiji Market. Cherry blossoms and ryokan experiences.

Italy

Rome – Colosseum, Vatican, Trevi Fountain, Pantheon. Also Florence, Venice, Amalfi Coast.

Spain

Madrid – Prado Museum, Royal Palace. Barcelona – Sagrada Familia, Park Güell, La Rambla. Tapas & Flamenco.

Mexico

Mexico City: Teotihuacan pyramids, Zócalo, Frida Kahlo Museum, Xochimilco. Cancún beaches, Mayan ruins.

South Africa

Cape Town: Table Mountain, Robben Island, Cape of Good Hope. Safari in Kruger, Garden Route, Stellenbosch wine.

Thailand

Bangkok: Grand Palace, Wat Arun, floating markets, Chatuchak Market. Islands like Phuket, Koh Samui, Chiang Mai temples.

Egypt

Pyramids of Giza, Egyptian Museum, Nile cruises, Valley of the Kings, Red Sea diving.

Greece

Athens Acropolis, Parthenon, Plaka district. Island hopping: Santorini, Mykonos, Crete. Mediterranean cuisine.
